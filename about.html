About Atia General Hospital

Affordable, patient-centered care for the people of Malir and Karachi, guided by clear standards and respect for every patient.

Vision & Mission

What we work towards, and how we get there.

👁️

Our Vision

Healthier communities through compassionate care, innovative research and inclusive education.

🎯

Our Mission

Delivering affordable, patient-centered, best health care, education, research and community-focused care, reinvesting resources to achieve our vision.

Quality Policy

Atia General Hospital is committed to:

  • Providing personalized, high-quality services according to each patient's individual needs.
  • Complying with the standards and regulations of the National and Sindh Healthcare Commission.
  • Valuing your suggestions and feedback so we can improve the services our hospital provides.

Patient Safety

How we keep every patient protected.

🧼

Infection Control

We follow strict standards of cleanliness and hygiene so patients are protected from germs. Every area is cleaned regularly.

💊

Correct Medication

The quantity and type of medicines given to patients are checked. Staff make sure medicines are used correctly so they have a positive effect on the patient's health.

🚑

Immediate Medical Aid

Procedures and emergency services have been improved for immediate treatment. Staff are always ready to give help without delay.

💬

Better Communication

We ensure clear and effective communication between patients and medical staff. Patients are told clearly about their condition, treatment and other matters.

Patient Rights

What every patient can expect from us.

ℹ️

Access to Information

Patients receive complete and accurate information about their health condition and treatment, which helps them make their own decisions.

🔒

Privacy Protection

The confidentiality of personal information is maintained. Health information is kept safe from any unauthorized person.

✅

Choice of Treatment

Patients have the right to choose treatment according to their own wishes, and can decide after learning about the different options.

🤝

Respect and Dignity

Every patient is treated with dignity and respect. Our staff take care of patients' needs and feelings.

Your feedback matters

We need your opinion to improve the facilities provided by the hospital.
